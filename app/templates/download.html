iOS

iPhone and iPad: install Sur Music, free

No download and no App Store needed. It opens full screen with its own icon, like an app.

  1. Open surmusicradio.com in Safari (it must be Safari, not another browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Sur Music from your Home Screen. Pick a station and press play.

Tips: keep the volume up and the silent switch off. Apple decides how long web audio may play with the screen locked; if the music stops, open the app and press play again. iPhones cannot install the Android app file (APK).

15 radio player themes, equalizer, voice commands and sleep timer

The big Now Playing screen in Sur Music has 15 themes, each with its own look and layout, not just a new colour: Aurora Glass, Mint Light, Rose Soft Card, Neon Tokyo, Cyber HUD, Deep Ocean, Emerald Ring, Golden Turntable, Sunset Poster, Arctic Crystal, Galaxy Orbit, Lava Bold, Desert Dunes, Monsoon Rain and Retro Cassette. Three themes are free for everyone on this website. All 15 are open in the Android app.

  • Equalizer, bass boost and loudness in the Android app, with presets such as Bass Boost, Vocal, Qawwali, Classical and Treble.
  • Voice commands in English, Urdu and Hindi, for example “Naat radio lagao”, “Quran chalao”, “Agla” or “Awaz barhao”. No extra account or paid service is needed.
  • Modern streaming engine (Google Media3 ExoPlayer) in the Android app: plays live news radio, HLS (.m3u8) channels, AAC and MP3 streams, and follows redirects, so more live news, talk and music stations start and keep playing in the background.
  • Sleep timer from 15 minutes to 1.5 hours: the radio stops by itself, even with the screen off.

Why use the apps?

  • More stations, including ones that stream over plain HTTP (browsers block those on secure websites).
  • Music keeps playing in the background with notification and lock-screen controls (Android).
  • No ads inside the apps.
  • On iPhone and iPad, add the website to your Home Screen and use it like an app.
