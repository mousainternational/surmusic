iOS

iPhone and iPad: install Sur Music, free

No download and no App Store needed. It opens full screen with its own icon, like an app.

  1. Open surmusicradio.com in Safari (it must be Safari, not another browser).
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Sur Music from your Home Screen. Pick a station and press play.

Tips: keep the volume up and the silent switch off. Apple decides how long web audio may play with the screen locked; if the music stops, open the app and press play again. iPhones cannot install the Android app file (APK).

Why use the apps?

  • More stations, including ones that stream over plain HTTP (browsers block those on secure websites).
  • Music keeps playing in the background with notification and lock-screen controls (Android).
  • No ads inside the apps.
  • On iPhone and iPad, add the website to your Home Screen and use it like an app.
