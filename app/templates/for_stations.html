Free player for your radio station

Your station is already on Sur Music Radio. Put a free live player on your own website in one minute — no account, no fee, no ads inside the player.

What you get

  • A live player for your website with your logo and name. Listeners press play and hear you, on phones and computers.
  • Your own page on Sur Music where listeners from many countries find you, save you to favourites and share you on WhatsApp and Facebook.
  • Blind-friendly — the player works with screen readers (TalkBack, VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS).
  • Free for ever. We do not ask for money, a login or your stream details.

How to add the player (1 minute)

  1. Find your station with the search box below and open its page.
  2. Scroll to "Add … to your website" and copy the short code.
  3. Paste the code into your website (in WordPress: add a Custom HTML block). That is all.
