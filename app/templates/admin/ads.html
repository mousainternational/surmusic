How to start earning

  1. Put the website online on your own domain with HTTPS, and keep it running for a while with real visitors.
  2. Apply at adsense.google.com with your domain. Google reviews the site (often a few days to a few weeks).
  3. After approval, copy your publisher ID (ca-pub-…) here. The site's ads.txt updates automatically.
  4. In AdSense → Ads → By ad unit, create Display ads units and paste each data-ad-slot number below.
  5. In AdSense → Privacy & messaging, turn on the European regulations (GDPR) message — Google requires a certified consent message for visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland.
  6. Tick “Show ads” and save.

Never click your own ads or ask others to click them — Google bans accounts for invalid clicks.
